# Visual style & voice of the old sites — a starting point for the next design

This is a working reference, not a spec. It describes what the two previous
versions of aikidoricany.cz actually looked and sounded like, based on the
Wayback Machine captures used to rebuild `historie.html` (screenshots in
`images/historie/era1-2007.jpg`, `era2-2017.jpg`, `era3-2026.jpg`). The goal
isn't to recreate either one — it's to name what was actually there, so a new
direction can consciously keep, drop, or transform each piece rather than
guessing at "what an aikido club site should look like" from nothing.

Two eras, two very different visual personalities, one thread that survives
both.

---

## Era 1 — hand-coded, 2006–2017 (Petr Mašát)

**Colour.** A muddy, moody palette: deep indigo/navy fills the space outside
the content box, a slate-blue sidebar sits inside it, and the article column
itself is a pale cream/off-white. Headings and nav links are a mid-blue.
Nothing else — no accent colour, no red, no warmth beyond the photography.
It reads as *nocturnal* rather than *branded*.

**Typography.** A classic serif for headings (Georgia/Times-adjacent), plain
body text — and notably, **justified** paragraphs. That single choice gives
the whole site a "printed pamphlet" feel rather than a web feel; it's part of
why it reads as dated now, but it's also distinctive and worth naming rather
than silently discarding.

**Layout.** A fixed-width box (~620px), centred in the browser with dead
space on both sides — very much a 2000s convention. Left sidebar with a
short vertical list of nav links, content on the right. One-column body text,
sparse imagery.

**Mark.** A single illustrated banner: a torii gate line-drawing wrapping the
人 (person) kanji, silhouetted against a moonlit forest photo, with 合気道
rendered vertically in a brush-style face on the right. This exact graphic
(as an animated GIF) is the one asset that survives, unchanged, into every
later era — it is the closest thing this club has to an actual logo.

**Voice.** First-person plural, direct, unpolished — a bulletin board, not
marketing copy. Announcements stack indefinitely without ever being pruned;
by 2017 the homepage was a single page holding *eleven years* of dated posts.
Prices, phone numbers, and exact class times sit inline in prose rather than
in a table. Personal signatures close out announcements ("Hodně štěstí a
zdraví Vám přeje za občanské sdružení Aikido Kenkyukai Říčany"). Smileys and
exclamation marks appear unselfconsciously. It never once sounds like it's
trying to sell anything — it sounds like a group of people telling their own
community what's happening next.

## Era 2 — Wix, 2018–2026

**Colour.** Wix's own "blank template" defaults: near-white background,
black text, no defined brand palette at all. The one carried-over colour is
whatever's in uploaded photos. Compared to Era 1's moodiness, this reads as
*generic* — it could be any small business on Wix.

**Typography.** Default Wix sans-serif for body copy; a slightly heavier
serif-ish face for the big hero statement ("Vítejte v Dojo 'Na Cestě'").
Nav items in the current version are set in wide-tracked uppercase — a
template flourish, not a considered choice.

**Layout.** Full-width sections, generous whitespace, everything centred —
the standard one-page Wix flow. The homepage becomes a single long scroll:
hero statement at top, "Aktuality" feed of dated posts-with-photos below,
replacing Era 1's sidebar+column split entirely. A soft wavy divider under
the hero shows up as a decorative template flourish in the most recent
capture. A member-login affordance ("Přihlásit se") appears — the club
briefly ran a Wix Forum, which Era 1 never had any equivalent of.

**Mark.** The same torii/kanji GIF, now just cropped into a small circle in
the header — present, but shrunk to an afterthought rather than a banner.

**Imagery.** Denser than Era 1 — photos attached to nearly every post — but
inconsistently sized and often uncropped straight-off-the-phone uploads.
Notably, at least one AI-generated image (`ChatGPT Image ....png`) appears
mixed in among real event photography in the most recent captures — a small
but real crack in visual consistency that's worth being deliberate about
either embracing or ruling out.

**Voice.** The same communal, first-person tone survives, but more
fragmented — shorter posts, more line breaks, occasional stray formatting
artefacts from the WYSIWYG editor (empty zero-width-space lines, inconsistent
bold). One new register appears that Era 1 never had: a fixed, aspirational
"manifesto" block about voluntary membership fees — *"Přijďte se k nám
protáhnout, posílit nebo jen na chvíli zastavit a být"* — warmer and more
composed than the logistics-heavy announcement voice around it. It reads
like two different people wrote it.

## What survives across all twenty years

- **The torii + 人 + 合気道 mark.** The only true piece of brand identity
  this club has ever had. Everything else changed around it.
- **First-person, communal, unpolished voice.** Never corporate, never
  salesy, in either era.
- **An accumulating record of small human events** — camps, cake contests,
  guest seminars, city grants — treated as worth writing down, not filtered
  into a "highlights" page. The value is in the accumulation itself.
- **A quiet philosophical undercurrent** (aikido as harmony/way, not
  competition) that shows up in the text in both eras even when nothing in
  the visual design reflects it.

## What's probably not worth carrying forward

- The fixed-width, justified-text, 2000s-boxed-page layout of Era 1 — reads
  as dated and is genuinely harder to read, especially on mobile.
- Era 1's low-contrast indigo-on-slate palette — moody, but an accessibility
  problem, not just a style choice.
- Era 2's generic unbranded Wix-default look — nothing in it says "this
  specific dojo" rather than "any small business that made a free website."
  This is the biggest single thing to fix.
- WYSIWYG formatting noise and ad hoc mixed-in AI imagery — worth a
  deliberate decision either way, not an accident.

## Seeds for wherever the new design goes

- The torii/kanji mark deserves to be a real, cleaned-up logo again — not a
  banner-sized illustration, not a shrunk-down GIF circle, something in
  between that can scale.
- The "moonlit forest" mood of the Era 1 banner — dark, night-time, quiet —
  is a real visual idea worth testing against something with actual contrast
  and a defined accent colour, rather than being abandoned entirely for
  Wix's blank-page brightness.
- The bulletin-board voice (dated entries, personal signatures, unfiltered
  accumulation) is a genuine texture worth protecting on purpose in any
  rewritten copy — not smoothing it into generic marketing English-via-Czech.
- Somewhere between Era 1's "no colour but mood" and Era 2's "no colour at
  all," an actual defined palette + accent colour is the one thing neither
  old site ever had and the new one should.
